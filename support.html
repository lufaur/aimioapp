Getting started

How do I pair my TV?

Make sure your iPhone and your TV are on the same Wi-Fi network, then open Aimio — it scans automatically and lists any compatible TV it finds. Tap it to pair. Most brands pair instantly; some (like Samsung and LG) show a one-time confirmation prompt on the TV screen the first time.

Which TV brands does Aimio support?

Roku, Samsung (Tizen), LG (webOS), Vizio (SmartCast), Sony (IRCC-IP), VIDAA — used by Hisense and Toshiba — and Android/Google TV. Apple TV and Amazon Fire TV are not supported.

My TV isn't showing up. What do I check?

Confirm your phone and TV are on the same Wi-Fi network (not one on Wi-Fi and one on a guest network or Ethernet-only VLAN), that the TV is powered on, and that Local Network access is allowed for Aimio in Settings → Privacy & Security → Local Network. Some routers isolate wireless clients from each other ("AP/client isolation") — if your router has that setting, turning it off for your home network usually fixes discovery.

Subscriptions & purchases

What's the difference between the annual plan and the lifetime purchase?

The annual plan is $14.99/year with a 7-day free trial and renews automatically. The lifetime purchase is $39.99 once, with no renewal — you own Aimio Pro permanently on your Apple ID. Both unlock the exact same features.

How do I cancel my subscription?

Subscriptions are managed entirely by Apple. Go to Settings → [your name] → Subscriptions on your iPhone, select Aimio, and turn off auto-renew. Aimio itself has no way to cancel a subscription directly, since Apple — not Aimio — processes the billing.

I reinstalled Aimio or got a new phone — do I have to pay again?

No. Open Aimio and tap Restore Purchases in Settings. It reapplies any purchase already tied to your Apple ID at no additional charge.

Can I get a refund?

Refunds are issued by Apple, not by Aimio directly. Request one at reportaproblem.apple.com.

Accessibility

Does Aimio work with VoiceOver?

Yes — every button and status indicator in Aimio has a VoiceOver label. Aimio also supports Switch Control, Voice Control, and Dynamic Type up to the largest accessibility sizes.

Is there a high-contrast mode?

Yes. Aimio includes a dedicated high-contrast skin that switches every button to pure black-on-white (or white-on-black in Dark Mode) for maximum legibility, and it also activates automatically if you have Increase Contrast turned on system-wide.

Privacy

Does Aimio send my data anywhere?

No. Aimio talks directly to your TV over your own Wi-Fi network — there's no server in between, no account, and no analytics or advertising SDKs. See the full Privacy Policy for details.
